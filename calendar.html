Institutional Pathways

Four Core Capability-Building Streams

Our continuous learning program is categorized into four strategic streams designed to elevate research rigor and societal impact.

Health & Clinical Sciences

Specialized in clinical protocols, hospital and laboratory research, epidemiological sampling, and quantitative health outcome assessments across MCNP programs.

Nursing & MedTech Pharmacy & RadTech Physical Therapy

Multidisciplinary & Social Sciences

Focusing on crime prevention, law enforcement policies, computing software architecture, education pedagogy, and entrepreneurship research across ISAP colleges.

Criminal Justice Computer Studies (CICT) Business & Education

Ethics Review & Publication

Administering PHREB Level 1 human participant ethics clearance, Turnitin digital originality checks (≤15%), IMRAD structure refinement, and OJS submission workflows.

PHREB Level 1 Turnitin Audits Double-Blind Peer Review

Grants Packaging & Utilization

Guiding proponents in securing competitive external research funding from DOST, CHED, and CVHRDC, followed by policy translation and municipal extension rollouts.

DOST / PCHRD Grants CHED Line-Item Budget Policy Briefs & Extension
