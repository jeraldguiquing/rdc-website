Our Commitment to the SDGs

One Research Community, 17 Global Goals, Sustainable Impact

Guided by the Mission, Vision, and Goals of the Medical Colleges of Northern Philippines (MCNP) and the International School of Asia and the Pacific (ISAP), the Research and Development Center (RDC) affirms its steadfast commitment to advancing the United Nations’ 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through research excellence, responsible innovation, knowledge utilization, and meaningful societal engagement.

Unified Institutional Pathway

Connecting Institutional Research with Humanity and the Planet

Together, ASCEND 2030 and INNOVATES 2026–2030 establish a unified pathway for advancing the SDGs. ASCEND provides the strategic direction, INNOVATES defines the research priorities, and the SDGs provide a global framework for connecting institutional research with the broader needs of humanity and the planet.

Active Institutional Force

Making Research an Active Force in Achieving the SDGs

Our commitment is not simply to align research with the Sustainable Development Goals, but to make research an active force in achieving them. We envision an academic community where every investigation yields tangible transformation:

Meaningful Concern

Every research question reflects a meaningful concern rooted in genuine community and global needs.

Credible Evidence

Every investigation generates credible evidence through rigorous methodology and ethical governance.

Opportunities for Innovation

Every discovery creates opportunities for innovation, responsible technology, and novel solutions.

Transform Communities

Every research output holds the potential to improve lives, strengthen institutions, and transform communities.

“Through this shared direction, the RDC endeavors to translate the institutions’ mission into purposeful research, vision into transformative innovation, and goals into measurable and sustainable impact.”

“For research must go beyond the pursuit of knowledge. It must confront challenges, inspire solutions, empower communities, and help shape a future where progress is inclusive, development is sustainable, and no one is left behind.”
