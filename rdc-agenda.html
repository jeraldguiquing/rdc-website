Institutional Blueprint (2025–2030)

01 • I: INSPIRE Lead: Teacher Education & Allied Health UN SDG 4: Quality Education NHERA Priority Track

INSPIRE: Pedagogical Dynamics, Simulation Learning & Educational Equity

Measuring learning outcomes in high-fidelity medical and criminology simulation laboratories, gamified learning modules, teacher competence metrics, outcome-based curricula, and inclusive education programs across diverse learning environments in Cagayan Valley.

Priority Sub-topics: Virtual OSCE (Objective Structured Clinical Examination) assessments; simulation stress tolerance; multilingual education in Cagayan schools; digital pedagogical integration; educator continuing professional development.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 6 (Health Sciences Education, Practice and Administration) & Area 7 (School Administration and Management).
02 • N: NURTURE Lead: ISAP Criminology Wing UN SDG 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions DOST R&D Priority

NURTURE: Public Safety, Forensic Science, Ethics & Penological Modernization

Developing empirical crime prevention matrices for rural municipalities, forensic ballistic imaging algorithms, restorative jail rehabilitative frameworks, human rights compliance, and comparative juvenile justice assessments.

Priority Sub-topics: Community policing trust metrics; questioned document fraud analysis; digital evidence handling under Philippine criminal procedures; correctional rehabilitation frameworks; bioethics in human subject studies.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 3 (Health Ethics & Institutional Governance) & Area 8 (Social Awareness and Response).
03 • N: NAVIGATE Joint: MCNP & ISAP UN SDG 9: Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure DICT & DOST Aligned

NAVIGATE: Digital Transformation, Cyber Forensics & Applied Informatics

Advancing artificial intelligence in healthcare triage, cybercrime threat analytics, tele-radiology systems for remote Cagayan islands, automated evaluation instruments, and secure institutional information frameworks.

Priority Sub-topics: Predictive clinical diagnostics; ransomware detection for regional organizations; automated evaluation tool design; AI-driven learning analytics; tele-health connectivity in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas (GIDAs).
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 12 (Development of Evaluation Tools) & Area 13 (Ergonomic Adaptability & Digital Infrastructures).
04 • O: OPERATIONALIZE Joint: MCNP & ISAP UN SDG 9: Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure DOST Regional Priority

OPERATIONALIZE: Applied Innovation, Systems Optimization & Tech Commercialization

Translating laboratory breakthroughs and conceptual prototypes into operational technologies, patented models, workflow enhancements, ergonomic adaptations, and community-deployable equipment.

Priority Sub-topics: Ergonomic hospital and classroom infrastructure design; low-cost diagnostic tooling; technology transfer and IP commercialization protocols; automated operational scheduling; pharmaceutical formulation scaling.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 4 (Drug Discovery) & Area 13 (Ergonomic Adaptability Infrastructures).
05 • V: VALUE Lead: MCNP Health Wing UN SDG 3: Good Health & Well-Being NUHRA / CVHRDC Track 1

VALUE: Clinical Healthcare Innovation, Disease Surveillance & Patient Well-Being

Targeting endemic disease epidemiology in Northern Luzon, antimicrobial stewardship, community maternal and pediatric diagnostics, radiologic dose reduction techniques, hospital clinical safety protocols, and holistic health across the lifespan.

Priority Sub-topics: Phytochemical characterization of native medicinal plants; hospital-acquired infection containment; community point-of-care diagnostics; pharmacovigilance and drug safety monitoring; alternative and traditional healthcare validation.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 1 (Health Across Life Span), Area 10 (Pharmacovigilance), & Area 11 (Alternative and Traditional Health Care).
06 • A: ADVANCE Joint: SDG Advocacies Synergy UN SDG 13: Climate Action SDG 11: Sustainable Communities

ADVANCE: Cagayan River Basin Eco-Resilience, Environmental Protection & Climate Action

Investigating flood risk mitigation along the Cagayan River, post-calamity waterborne disease prevention, food security in farming villages, biodiversity conservation, and community resilience during hydrometeorological disasters.

Priority Sub-topics: Water purification methods for flood-prone barangays; disaster triage readiness among first responders; indigenous ecological coping mechanisms; watershed soil conservation; climate-resilient community health models.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 2 (Environmental Protection and Conservation) & Area 8 (Social Awareness and Response).
07 • T: TRANSFORM Lead: Community Extension & Social Sciences UN SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities SDG 1: No Poverty

TRANSFORM: Community Empowerment, Social Inclusion & Public Welfare

Bridging institutional research with grassroots community needs through participatory action research, livelihood support assessments, indigenous peoples' welfare programs, public health outreach evaluation, and civic empowerment.

Priority Sub-topics: Rural health literacy programs; indigenous education and cultural preservation; youth diversion and restorative justice in barangays; participatory needs assessment matrices; social vulnerability indices.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 5 (Health Programs Assessment and Evaluation) & Area 8 (Social Awareness and Response).
08 • E: EXPAND Joint: External Affairs & RDC Directorate UN SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals

EXPAND: Strategic Consortia, Institutional Linkages & Global Collaboration

Fostering inter-institutional research networks, cross-border academic consortia, regional industry linkages, alumni research integration, and multi-agency health and technology research consortiums (including CVHRDC, DOST 02, and CHED RO2).

Priority Sub-topics: Regional consortium data harmonization; international faculty and student research exchanges; alumni tracer and research engagement studies; collaborative cross-sectoral grant acquisition; public-private research partnerships.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 9 (Alumni Engagement & Tracking) & Area 17 (Partnership Frameworks).
09 • S: STRENGTHEN Lead: Customs Administration & Business Management UN SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth SDG 16: Peace & Strong Institutions

STRENGTHEN: Trade Logistics, Institutional Governance & Policy Modernization

Analyzing trade corridors connecting Port of Irene, Port of Aparri, and East Asian economic zones, SME survival strategies in Cagayan Valley, institutional research governance, program evaluation frameworks, and ethical corporate management.

Priority Sub-topics: Customs digital clearance bottleneck analysis; agricultural supply chain financing; women-led micro-enterprises in Peñablanca; institutional program assessment matrices; quality management systems (QMS) in higher education.
• Aligned with Institutional Research Priorities: Area 5 (Health & Institutional Programs Assessment) & Area 7 (School Administration and Management).
