Evidence-Based Extension Caravans

Students and faculty step into partner barangays to conduct baseline surveys, health screenings, and needs assessments. Every study informs actual municipal public health programs and community extension services.

Data Literacy & Infographics Competitions

Students sharpen critical thinking and data interpretation skills through the annual Statistical Month competitions, transforming raw epidemiological and social data into compelling visual narratives.
